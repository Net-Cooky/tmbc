Who are we?

Founded in 2013 by John Reardon, The Mobile Billboard Company was born from a vision to fill a gap in the Australian media landscape—offering businesses a more targeted, hands-on, and locally focused marketing solution. Unlike traditional media, mobile billboards provide a dynamic and highly impactful way to reach audiences directly.

With a team boasting over 30 years of collective experience in Australian marketing and media, we bring deep expertise across Out-of-Home (OOH), Television, Digital, and Online advertising—helping brands cut through the noise and make a lasting impression.
